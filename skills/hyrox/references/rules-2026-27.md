# HYROX rules for the 2026/27 season

Read this file before you state any rule, load, standard, or penalty. Every rule
here comes from one of the four official English rulebooks for Season 26/27. Rules
change each season. Before a race, tell the athlete to check the current official
rulebook for their format, because HYROX can publish a new copy without a new
season label.

## Which documents

| Short name in this skill | Official document | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| Singles 26/27 | HYROX Rulebook Singles, Season 26/27, English | 46 |
| Doubles 26/27 | HYROX Rulebook Doubles, Season 26/27, English | 48 |
| Relay 26/27 | HYROX Rulebook Relay, Season 26/27, English | 35 |
| Adaptive 26/27 | HYROX Rulebook Adaptive, Season 26/27, English | 56 |

A citation such as "(Singles 26/27 p. 8)" means page 8 of that PDF. The download
addresses are in [sources.md](sources.md).

- No rulebook prints a version number or a publication date. The only printed
  label is "SEASON 26/27" (Singles 26/27 p. 1 and the same page in the others).
- The Adaptive book says Classification Codes arrive "By the mid-season" and that
  "a mid-season review will take place" (Adaptive 26/27 pp. 52, 54). Adaptive rules
  may change during the season.
- When you save an analysis that depends on a rule, write the season and the
  rulebook in the record: in the `org.athleticstandard/hyrox-race` extension for a
  result, and in the text of a note or review.

## Race format

- "HYROX consists of a 1 km run followed by 1 station, repeated 8 times." (Singles
  26/27 p. 4; Doubles, Relay, and Adaptive p. 4)
- Runs and stations are done in order: run 1, station 1, run 2, station 2, and so
  on, for 8 km of running and 8 stations (Singles 26/27 p. 22).
- A run segment is "approximately 1,000 meters" and may be several laps. The first
  or last lap may be a different length; the difference is made up inside that
  segment so the full 8,000 m is run (Singles 26/27 p. 23).
- Racers count their own laps. Lap screens are only an aid. Extra laps get no time
  back (Singles 26/27 p. 22).
- Fast Lane: "Racers running at a 4/km pace or faster are required to use the Fast
  Lane" (Singles 26/27 pp. 23–24). The book prints no unit. It most likely means
  4 minutes per km.
- No book sets an overall time limit, a station time cap, or a mid-race cut-off.

## Divisions and loads

All loads for sleds include the sled. Colours are the colours of the race
equipment.

### Singles (Singles 26/27 p. 8, pp. 27–35)

Four divisions: Women, Women Pro, Men, Men Pro. "Open" in this skill means the
non-Pro Women and Men divisions.

| Station | Women | Men | Women Pro | Men Pro |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SkiErg | 1,000 m | 1,000 m | 1,000 m | 1,000 m |
| Sled Push | 102 kg | 152 kg | 152 kg | 202 kg |
| Sled Pull | 78 kg | 103 kg | 103 kg | 153 kg |
| Burpee Broad Jumps | 80 m | 80 m | 80 m | 80 m |
| Rowing | 1,000 m | 1,000 m | 1,000 m | 1,000 m |
| Farmers Carry, 200 m | 2 × 16 kg (white) | 2 × 24 kg (grey) | 2 × 24 kg (grey) | 2 × 32 kg (black) |
| Sandbag Lunges, 100 m | 10 kg (white) | 20 kg (grey) | 20 kg (grey) | 30 kg (black) |
| Wall Balls, 100 reps | 4 kg (white) | 6 kg (grey) | 6 kg (grey) | 9 kg (black) |
| Wall-ball target height | 2.70 m | 3.00 m | 2.70 m | 3.00 m |

Every Singles division does 100 wall balls. The seeded `hyrox-full` benchmark
definition in a new Athletic Standard database says "wall balls 100/75 reps". That
text predates the 2026/27 rules. Use this table, not that text.

### Doubles (Doubles 26/27 p. 8, pp. 28–37)

Five divisions: Doubles Women, Doubles Women Pro, Doubles Men, Doubles Men Pro,
Doubles Mixed. A Mixed team is one woman and one man (Doubles 26/27 p. 22).

| Station | Doubles Women | Doubles Men, Women Pro, Mixed | Doubles Men Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sled Push | 102 kg | 152 kg | 202 kg |
| Sled Pull | 78 kg | 103 kg | 153 kg |
| Farmers Carry | 2 × 16 kg | 2 × 24 kg | 2 × 32 kg |
| Sandbag Lunges | 10 kg | 20 kg | 30 kg |
| Wall Balls | 4 kg | 6 kg | 9 kg |

SkiErg, Rowing, Burpee Broad Jumps, and the 100 wall-ball reps are the same as
Singles. Wall-ball targets: women 2.70 m, men 3.00 m (Doubles 26/27 p. 37). The
book says "female racers must hit their target in the strike zone, male racers
must hit their target in the strike zone" (Doubles 26/27 p. 36). For a Mixed team
it does not say in one sentence which target each partner uses; the wording
suggests each partner throws to the target for their own sex with the 6 kg ball.

### Relay (Relay 26/27 p. 8, pp. 16–24)

Three divisions: Women, Men, Mixed. A team is 4 women, 4 men, or 2 women and 2 men
(Relay 26/27 p. 10). There is no Pro Relay.

| Station | Women | Men |
|---|---|---|
| Sled Push | 102 kg | 152 kg |
| Sled Pull | 78 kg | 103 kg |
| Farmers Carry | 2 × 16 kg | 2 × 24 kg |
| Sandbag Lunges | 10 kg | 20 kg |
| Wall Balls | 4 kg to 2.70 m | 6 kg to 3.00 m |

The table has no Mixed column. The book does not say which loads a Mixed relay
team uses (Relay 26/27 p. 8). Tell the athlete to ask the organiser.

### Adaptive

Thirteen divisions with their own distances, loads, and standards (Adaptive 26/27
pp. 9–41). See [adaptive.md](adaptive.md). Never assign one.

### Pro, Elite 15, and Challenger

Pro divisions exist in Singles and Doubles. Elite 15 and the Challenger Division
are invitation races drawn from Pro rankings (Singles 26/27 pp. 14–21). The books do
not list separate Elite loads.

## General station rules

- Each station "must be completed in full. Any failure to complete a workout
  station in its entirety will result in disqualification [Incomplete Station]."
  (Singles 26/27 p. 25; Doubles p. 26; Relay p. 14). Adaptive uses time penalties
  instead (Adaptive 26/27 pp. 23–41).
- Judges assign equipment and lanes. "Racers are not permitted to choose their own
  equipment or lane under any circumstances." (Singles 26/27 p. 25)
- A movement that "deviates from the prescribed standards, appears unusual or
  uncommon, or appears to provide a competitive advantage will not be recognised"
  (Singles 26/27 p. 25).
- Stations 1–7: one warning per station; from the second fault, each fault costs
  the listed time penalty. Some faults are penalised without warning (Singles 26/27
  p. 44). Wall balls give no warnings: "it is either a rep or a no-rep." (Singles
  26/27 p. 36)

Station-by-station standards, faults, and penalties are in
[stations.md](stations.md).

Other penalties stated in the text:

- Kettlebells or sandbag returned to the wrong place: no penalty if fixed "before
  exiting the Roxzone" (Singles 26/27 pp. 32–33).
- Wrong weight at farmers carry or lunges: repeat the whole station with the right
  weight, or be disqualified (Singles 26/27 pp. 32–33).
- Using equipment, a lane, or a wall-ball station not assigned to you: 2 min each
  (Singles 26/27 p. 23).
- Doubles partner running ahead: 1 min; more than 3 such penalties means Out Of
  Competition (Doubles 26/27 p. 25).
- Adaptive has its own table; see [adaptive.md](adaptive.md).
- "Penalties may be added or amendments made for up to 48 hours after each event
  ends." (Singles 26/27 p. 10). Check the result again after 48 hours before you
  log penalties as final.

## Result statuses

- **DQ:** no result data, no ranking, and the racer may not continue (Singles 26/27
  p. 44). Only Race Directors disqualify.
- **DNF:** a racer who "does not finish a station" gets no results data "However,
  they may continue with the race [without a final time]" (Singles 26/27 p. 44).
- **DNS:** not wearing the timing chip while racing (Singles 26/27 p. 22).
- **Out of Competition:** any change to movements, weights, or reps for pregnancy
  or injury. The racer gets a finish time but no ranking (Singles 26/27 pp. 5–6).
  Pregnancy changes must be arranged with HYROX in advance. Injured racers need
  medical clearance (Singles 26/27 p. 6).
- **Out of Ranking:** HYROX may remove an event or part of it from qualification
  when venue or weather affects performance or safety (Singles 26/27 p. 10).

## Roxzone and timing

The rulebooks do not define the Roxzone. They do not say where it starts or ends,
and they do not list official split types. What they do say:

- The course combines "the Run Course, Roxzone and workout station distances"
  (Singles 26/27 p. 23).
- The Roxzone has IN and OUT arches. Using them the wrong way costs 2 min each time
  (Singles 26/27 p. 43).
- Each racer wears a timing chip "on an ankle at all times" (Singles 26/27 p. 41).
  Many penalties "are automatically detected by the timing system using chip reads"
  (Singles 26/27 p. 43).
- Results are published at results.hyrox.com right after the event (Singles 26/27
  p. 10).

So split fields such as run times, station times, and a Roxzone total come from the
timing provider, not the rulebook. Layouts differ. Always record which layout a
result came from. See [race-analysis.md](race-analysis.md).

## Kit, chalk, water, and food

- Allowed: knee sleeves, gloves (not grips), a weightlifting belt, wristbands,
  hydration packs, prescribed inhalers (Singles 26/27 p. 38). "Any item not
  explicitly listed as permitted is, by default, prohibited." (Singles 26/27
  pp. 38–39)
- Not allowed: headphones, phones, cameras, smart glasses, ear plugs, and similar
  (Singles 26/27 p. 39). Weight vests and uniforms are allowed if they do not
  affect others (Singles 26/27 p. 39).
- Closed-toe shoes at all times, except that shoes may come off at wall balls
  (Singles 26/27 p. 38).
- Chalk: event chalk only, only at sled pull and farmers carry (Singles 26/27
  p. 45).
- Water is available "at least once during, before or after each pass through the
  Roxzone" (Singles 26/27 p. 40). Racers who want food must carry it from the start.
  No food or drink from anyone except aid stations (Singles 26/27 p. 40).
- Be at the start tunnel 10 minutes before the start time (Singles 26/27 p. 42).

## Age groups

- Singles and Doubles: under 24 (16–24), then five-year groups to 85–89 (Singles
  26/27 p. 9). A Doubles team's group is the partners' average age on race day
  (Doubles 26/27 p. 10).
- Relay: under 40 and 40+, by the team's average age (Relay 26/27 p. 9).
- Minimum age: 16 on race day (Singles 26/27 p. 5).

## Gaps and contradictions in the rulebooks

Say so when an answer depends on one of these. Tell the athlete to ask the
organiser when it matters on race day.

1. No version number or date is printed; only "SEASON 26/27".
2. The Roxzone and official split types are not defined.
3. An incomplete station is called DQ in one place, while a DNF racer "may
   continue" in another (Singles 26/27 pp. 25, 44–45).
4. Doubles machines: the athlete may not leave the machine before the full
   distance, yet the "You Go I Go" example switches partners mid-row (Doubles 26/27
   pp. 24, 27, 32).
5. The Doubles age-group heading excludes Pro, but the text gives Pro teams
   age-group rankings (Doubles 26/27 p. 9).
6. Doubles World Championship slot counts are stated two ways (Doubles 26/27
   pp. 11–12).
7. "80+ Age Groups" is used, but the listed groups are 80–84 and 85–89.
8. The Doubles "not staying together" penalty is in the text but not in the Doubles
   penalty table (Doubles 26/27 pp. 25, 47).
9. Relay Mixed loads are not stated (Relay 26/27 p. 8).
10. Relay leg order is "entirely up to the team", yet the finish text names a final
    member and Run 8 (Relay 26/27 pp. 10, 25).
11. The Relay Transition Zone penalty size, the zone's location, and which chip read
    sets the team time are not stated (Relay 26/27 pp. 13, 25).
12. The Relay SkiErg section has no damper note; the other books preset it to 6.
13. Chalk is allowed "at the designated stations where it is provided" in one
    place and "JUST FOR SLED PULL AND FARMERS CARRY" in the table (Singles 26/27
    pp. 23, 45).
14. Adaptive digital wall-ball targets count reps; in the other books screens "do
    not determine the validity of repetitions" (Adaptive 26/27 p. 40; Singles p. 35).
15. Adaptive SkiErg and rower shortfall: 15 s per metre in the text, "MIN PENALTY
    10 SECS TO DQ" in the table (Adaptive 26/27 pp. 24, 32, 50).
16. Adaptive undeclared equipment: penalty or DQ on one page, "may adjust their
    rank" in the appendix (Adaptive 26/27 pp. 19, 56).
17. Adaptive: the classifier and process are not named, age groups are not listed,
    and "1PT" and "2PT" are not defined.
18. Adaptive "1 LAP" and "2 LAPS" have no metre value; they depend on the venue.
19. The burpee 30 cm measuring note sits under the wrong rule (Singles 26/27 p. 29).
20. Doubles uses "Both Doubles Teams" where it means both athletes.
21. Elite tier names differ between Singles and Doubles.
22. The Fast Lane "4/km" has no unit.
