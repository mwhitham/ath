## How these were checked

- Rulebook facts come from the HYROX rules notes, which read the four PDFs and give
  page numbers.
- HYROX studies come from the HYROX research notes. Brandt 2025 and Rappelt 2026 were
  read in full; the rest from abstracts (HRN 2, 9).
- Transferable studies come from section 5 of the HYROX research notes, which checked
  each against its abstract (HRN 10.2).
- Monitoring studies come from the calculation-methods notes.
- A study that is in none of these notes is not cited in this skill.
