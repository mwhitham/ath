## Not verified

- The full text of Foster et al. (2001) was not read. The 30-minute rating delay is
  reported by later papers.
- The 2027 CrossFit Games Rulebook is not published. The 2027 age groups differ
  between two official pages.
- WheelWOD's adaptive rulebook was not read. The full 2026 adaptive division list
  is not confirmed.
- No single sentence in the 2026 rulebook states how a capped individual Open score
  ranks. The rule in [benchmarks.md](benchmarks.md) comes from the scorecards, the
  penalty example, and the team rule.
- Fight Gone Bad scoring, wall-ball target heights for Kelly and Filthy 50, snatch
  style for Isabel and Amanda, and the sit-up type for Annie are not on the official
  pages.
- Jackie's first-posted date conflicts between crossfit.com (2005) and the CrossFit
  Journal (November 2004).
- Bosquet et al. (2007) and Currier et al. (2023) were read as abstracts only.
